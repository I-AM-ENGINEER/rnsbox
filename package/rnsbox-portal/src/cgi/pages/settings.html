NTP over Reticulum (rns-time)

When ordinary NTP is unreachable, step the clock from a time server on the Reticulum mesh. The server is authenticated by its destination hash (an encrypted RNS Link to that identity); accuracy is a few milliseconds over TCP/LAN and well under a second over LoRa. A board with an uplink can also serve time to the rest of the mesh.
