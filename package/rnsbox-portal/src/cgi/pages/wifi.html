Hotspot (access point)

Runs concurrently with the client connection (onboard WiFi single radio, time-shared). The hotspot is bridged with the USB-C link into one LAN (br-lan): WiFi clients join the same 10.42.0.0/24 subnet and DHCP pool as the USB host, reach this portal at 10.42.0.1, are NAT'd to the WAN, and talk to the USB host directly (switched, not firewalled).

__AP_PSK_NOTE__

The hotspot ships with no password and stays off until you choose one: 8–63 printable ASCII characters, without # or ". The old published default changeme123 is refused.

In concurrent sta+ap mode the client is pinned to 2.4 GHz and the hotspot inherits the client's exact channel and width (single radio — they must share one channel), so it runs at 40 MHz whenever the client link is 40 MHz. If the client isn't connected yet, the hotspot starts on the channel below and re-syncs to the client's channel once it joins. auto channel and a manually chosen 40 MHz apply only in AP-only mode; if the radio can't honor them the hotspot falls back to a 20 MHz channel.

TX power

Radio-wide — applies to both client and hotspot (single radio). auto = chip maximum, independent of the regulatory database. A number caps power; firmware still applies per-rate EVM backoff below it. The onboard chip (CYW43436 class) is a 2.4 GHz-only radio; the practical regulatory ceiling is 20 dBm EIRP in most domains. Set lower to cut heat/range.
