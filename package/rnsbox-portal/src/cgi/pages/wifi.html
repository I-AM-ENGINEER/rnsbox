Hotspot (access point)

Runs concurrently with the client connection (onboard WiFi single radio, time-shared). AP clients are NAT'd to the WAN and get DHCP from the AP subnet in /etc/rnsbox/wifi.conf.

In concurrent sta+ap mode the client is pinned to 2.4 GHz and the hotspot inherits the client's exact channel and width (single radio — they must share one channel), so it runs at 40 MHz whenever the client link is 40 MHz. If the client isn't connected yet, the hotspot starts on the channel below and re-syncs to the client's channel once it joins. auto channel and a manually chosen 40 MHz apply only in AP-only mode; if the radio can't honor them the hotspot falls back to a 20 MHz channel.

TX power

Radio-wide — applies to both client and hotspot (single radio). auto = chip maximum, independent of the regulatory database. A number caps power; firmware still applies per-rate EVM backoff below it. The onboard chip (CYW43436 class) is a 2.4 GHz-only radio; the practical regulatory ceiling is 20 dBm EIRP in most domains. 11b-CCK, 18 dBm MCS7), so values above ~18 only affect legacy 11b, not data-rate throughput. Set lower to cut heat/range.
